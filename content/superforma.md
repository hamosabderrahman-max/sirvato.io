# SUPERFORMA

The meta-framework. SUPERFORMA is the governing logic that makes SIRVATO coherent at every scale.

---

## What It Is

SUPERFORMA is not a design guide. It is the structural law that all SIRVATO output must satisfy.

It operates above content, above design, above individual components.

## The Three Pillars

### Unità
Everything is one. A single coherent system. No orphan pages. No contradictory signals. No duplicated meaning.

### Presenza
Every element is present and intentional. Nothing hides. Nothing is decorative without purpose. If something is on the page, it earns its place.

### Operatività
Output is operational, not theoretical. Every page, every component, every word must serve an immediate function.

## Enforcement

SUPERFORMA is enforced at build time. Any page that violates a pillar is rejected.

**Validation checklist:**
- Is every section readable in under 5 seconds?
- Is there zero redundancy?
- Is there zero conceptual noise?
- Does the page enable action?

## Relationship to Other Domains

SUPERFORMA does not belong to any single domain.
It is the lens through which all domains are evaluated.

---

> SUPERFORMA is the difference between a system and a collection of files.
